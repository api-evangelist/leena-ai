# Leena AI

Leena AI is an enterprise Agentic AI platform that deploys AI Colleagues to automate and run back-office work across IT, HR, Finance, Procurement, and Operations with measurable ROI.

The platform is trusted by **25M+ employees and deployed at 500+ global enterprises** across industries including beverages, healthcare, finance, telecom, media, education, and technology. Customers include Coca-Cola, Nestlé, MongoDB, Etihad, Swiggy, CIPLA, Godrej, Abbott and more.

---

## Core Concepts

### Agentic AI
Leena AI follows an Agentic AI architecture where autonomous and semi-autonomous AI Colleagues:
- Understand user intent
- Reason across enterprise context
- Take actions across systems
- Learn from outcomes
- Operate within governed guardrails

Agentic AI Architecture:
https://leena.ai/Agentic-AI-Architecture

---

## AI Colleagues

AI Colleagues are domain-specific, role-based AI agents that work like digital coworkers.

They are:
- Pre-trained for enterprise workflows
- Voice and chat enabled
- Capable of multi-step reasoning
- Integrated with enterprise systems
- Governed with approvals, policies, and audit trails

AI Colleagues Studio:
https://leena.ai/ai-colleagues-studio

---

## AI Colleagues Studio

AI Colleagues Studio is a no-code and low-code environment to:
- Customize existing AI Colleagues
- Build new AI Colleagues
- Define tools, actions, and workflows
- Configure policies, approvals, and escalation
- Monitor performance and outcomes

The Studio enables faster deployment of Agentic AI without development cycles of months.
